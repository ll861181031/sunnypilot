## Dataset
- 17,000 images: batch_001 (12,000) + batch_002_night (5,000)
- Route-safe split: 14,039 train / 2,961 val, no route overlap
- 17 classes: person, two_wheeler, tricycle, car, van, truck, bus, trailer,
  special_vehicle, stroller, wheelchair, animal, cone, barrier, traffic_light,
  traffic_sign, other_obstacle
- Labels: automatic prelabels cleaned by human corrections (149 edited frames,
  tricycle/rider propagation); not fully human-reviewed.

## Training
- Base: YOLO11x COCO (`yolo11x.pt`), 56.8M params, 194.9 GFLOPs
- 640 px, batch 16, AMP, freeze first 10 layers, cache=ram, 8 workers
- 30 epoch budget, early stop patience 8 -> stopped after 27 epochs
- Best checkpoint epoch 19; 2h03m total on RTX 4070 SUPER
- Augmentation: Ultralytics defaults (mosaic, HSV, flip, scale), close_mosaic 10

## Caveats
- The training labels are mostly auto-prelabels plus limited human corrections;
  mAP is measured against those labels, not an independent clean ground truth.
- `traffic_light` precision is limited by the remaining flag/sign false positives.
- `bus` recall is low (0.47).
- The model is AGPL-3.0 (Ultralytics YOLO11x weights/license).

## 实拍验证视频后的重要更正（2026-10-27 17:10）

- 已生成 `YOLO-model17-night-tricycle.mp4`（route 40 夜间）和
  `YOLO-model17-day-parked.mp4`（白天停车区），均为 30s/20fps/1344x760。
- 视频里 **夜间停放的三轮车仍被标成 car**。进一步检查：对 batch_002 全部 127 张
  含 tricycle 标签的图，模型在 conf=0.001 下仍 **0 个 tricycle 预测**；但 `val` 表却给出
  tricycle mAP50=0.769。两者矛盾，**验证表的 tricycle 指标很可能是组合数据集的重复
  6 位 ID / labels.cache 串批导致的**。
- 结论：**两轮车/人/小汽车/货车可用，三轮车尚未真正学会**；不要把当前模型用于三轮车验收。
- 下一步应先重建“无重复文件 ID”的组合数据集（例如 `b1_000001` / `n_000001`）或单批训练
  验证后重训，再重新导出；不要直接在当前指标上做部署决策。
